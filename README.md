### About

I'm a Chilean engineer who's spent over a decade at the seam between technology, design, and marketing — a background that lets me read a business the way a product person does and ship it the way an engineer does.

Today I work as a **Senior Software Engineer** with a heavy bias toward AI-augmented workflows and agent orchestration: plugging intelligence into the places where it actually moves metrics, not demo-ware. I've shipped across healthcare, OTT streaming, advertising, education, retail, and tourism — the thread that holds it together is the same every time: I'm fast at understanding the pain a business and the people inside it feel, and that speed, plus leadership, is my real differentiator. Code is a consequence.

- 🏥 Leading engineering for **Alemana Go** at Clínica Alemana de Santiago — the patient-facing platform for one of Chile's largest healthcare networks
- 🎓 Teaching Software Engineering at **IPSS** *(formerly CIISA)*
- ✍️ Writing about how AI is reshaping engineering teams at [dobleb.cl/blog](https://dobleb.cl/en/blog/)
- 🪦 Recently archived my own spec-driven-development methodology after actually shipping it — [wrote up why](https://dobleb.cl/en/blog/abandoning-tsdd/)
